CakesByIftu LLC Policies

  1. Booking & Payment
    • Orders are not confirmed until payment is received.
    • A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure most orders.
    • Wedding cakes $400 and under must be paid in full at booking.
    • Invoices must be paid within 48 hours of being sent or the date may be released and opened to others. I will reach out to you via your chosen contact method if I have any questions or need verification. Once all changes have been made and confirmed, your invoice will be sent.
    • Two reminders will also be sent: the day before it is due and the day it is due. If payment is not made by the due date, the order will be canceled. No payment can be made after.
    • For orders placed with less than 1 week notice, 50% is due by the end of that same day.
  2. Wedding Cake Booking
    • A minimum of 1 month (4 weeks) notice is required for all wedding cake bookings.
    • A non-refundable deposit must be paid no later than 3 weeks before the event date to secure your order.
    • Orders without a paid deposit by the 3-week deadline will be automatically canceled and the date released. No exceptions.
  3. Final Payment
    • Remaining balances must be paid by the date listed on the invoice.
    • For orders $400 and over, the remaining balance is due 2 weeks prior to the event, with a 48-hour payment deadline and reminders.
    • Full payment is required before pickup or delivery.
    • No cake will be released without full payment. No exceptions.
  4. Payment Methods
    • CakesByIftu is now sending invoices via Square Invoice.
    • Accepted payments: Square (debit/credit), Cash App Pay
  5. Cancellations
    • Deposits are non-refundable once an order is confirmed.
    • Orders canceled within 2 weeks of the event date are not eligible for refund.
    • Any orders canceled before 2 weeks of the event may be eligible for refund.
  6. Pickup & Delivery
    • Pickup address will be sent the day before your scheduled pickup.
    • Please arrive at the agreed pickup time. If you are running late or need to reschedule, you have 3 days to communicate or the order will not be held.
    • Any cake 6” + 8” + 10” tier or larger must be delivered.
  7. Allergy Notice
    • Clients must inform me of any allergies before booking.
    • My kitchen is not allergen-free, and I cannot guarantee products are free from cross-contamination.

CakesByIftu is not liable for any damages once the product is picked up or delivered.

CakesByIftu LLC is a registered business in the State of Minnesota and operates as a Minnesota Registered Cottage Food Producer following state food safety regulations.
