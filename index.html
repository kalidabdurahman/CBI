Order Agreement

Please read the following terms and conditions before placing your order:

  1. BOOKING & DEPOSITS
    • A 50% NON-REFUNDABLE DEPOSIT is required to confirm all orders.
    • Your order is NOT CONFIRMED until the deposit is received.
    • Deposit is only refundable if cancellation is made 2 WEEKS OR MORE before your pickup/delivery date.
    • For orders over $500, a 100% deposit is required 2 WEEKS IN ADVANCE of due date. If cancelled after that, only 50% will be refunded.
  2. FINAL PAYMENT
    • The remaining balance is due by the date stated on your invoice.
    • For orders over $500, a 5-DAY GRACE PERIOD after that date is allowed.
    • For pickups, payment must be made by the MORNING OF THE PICKUP DATE.
    • For deliveries, FULL PAYMENT MUST BE RECEIVED BEFORE DELIVERY. No deliveries without it.
    • If payment is not received, YOUR ORDER WILL NOT BE RELEASED. No exceptions.
  3. PAYMENT METHODS
    • Accepted for most orders: CashApp, Venmo, Apple Cash.
    • Required for orders over $500: Zelle or Apple Cash only.
    • Payment instructions will be provided in your invoice.
  4. CANCELLATIONS
    • Cancellations must follow the 2-week policy to receive a refund (see #1).
    • If you cancel outside that window, your deposit is forfeited.
    • NO CALL, NO SHOW: I will hold your cake for 2 days; without communication it will be discarded, no refund.
    • Any cake larger than a 6″ + 8″ + 10″ tier must be delivered—no exceptions.
  5. PICKUP POLICIES
    • Pickup address is sent the night before your pickup date.
    • Please respect the agreed time: you have a 15-MINUTE GRACE PERIOD without communication.
    • If you’re running late or need to reschedule, you have 3 days to communicate or the order will not be held.
  6. ALLERGIES
    • Please inform me of any allergies during booking. I do my best to avoid cross-contamination, but my workspace is not allergen-free.
  7. BUSINESS DETAILS
    • CakesByIftu is a registered LLC operating under a valid Minnesota tax ID. Sales are reported and taxes filed monthly.
    • All payments must be made as agreed—this is a legally operating business.
