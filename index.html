Layers vs. Tiers

A Small Distinction That Makes Cake Sizing Much Easier.

CAKE

The Inside

What Is a Layer?

Every CakesByIftu tier contains 3 layers of cake separated by 2 layers of filling.

The Stack

What Is a Tier?

A tier is one complete cake. A three-tier wedding cake has three separate cakes stacked together — each with 3 cake layers.

Layers are inside a cake. Tiers are the cakes stacked on top of one another.

Decorative Options

Real vs. Dummy Tiers

Real tiers are fully edible. Dummy tiers are decorative, non-edible tiers used to create height or a larger appearance. A combination of both is also possible. Please mention that in your initial inquiry.

Dummy tiers still require materials, decorating, and labor, so they are not automatically cheaper.

Wedding Cake 101 / 07

Flavors & Fillings

Beautiful Cakes Need Flavors That Work Well Together — and Stay Stable.

Make It Yours

Your Flavor Options

Cake flavors and fillings can be selected for different tiers, depending on the design and structural needs of the cake.

Buttercream fillings are often more stable for stacked cakes.

Under Flavors are all the available cake flavors and filling options. You are also able to mix and match each of the tiers.

Important to Know

A Note About Softer Fillings

Slippery or soft fillings—such as loose fruit compotes, jams, and some cream fillings—may not be recommended for the bottom tiers of tall wedding cakes.

Under pressure, they can shift if not properly stabilized. Filling placement is chosen with stability and food safety in mind.
